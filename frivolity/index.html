Common questions

Where did my high score go?
Records are saved on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, to your Apple Account — so a new iPhone picks them up. They are lost if you delete the app and are not signed in to iCloud.

How do the bouquets work?
Fill one of the nine 3×3 beds, a full row or a full column and the nine flowers growing there are cut into a bouquet of exactly those blooms. The beds are the nine boxes marked out by the deeper furrows, as in sudoku; any other 3×3 patch does not count. Clear more than one at once for a bonus.

How do I get on the leaderboard?
Open the bouquet shelf and turn on Post scores to Game Center. Your best score so far goes straight onto the leaderboard, with no need to beat it again, and every game after that counts toward the weekly board. You need to be signed in to Game Center in the Settings app. Leaderboards arrive in version 1.2.

Can I turn the pieces?
No — pieces are placed as they come. That is the puzzle.

Where does the plant information come from?
Every hardiness zone, bloom season and planting depth in the field guide is taken from a named source — chiefly the NC State Extension Plant Toolbox, Iowa State University Extension, Clemson Cooperative Extension and the Missouri Botanical Garden — and each entry names the page it came from.

The ads.
Frivolity is free and shows an occasional ad between games. Never during a game, and never in the field guide.
